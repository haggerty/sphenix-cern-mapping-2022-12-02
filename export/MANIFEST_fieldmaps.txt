sPHENIX solenoid field maps — CERN 2022-12-02 mapping
=====================================================
Staged: 2026-06-09 (John Haggerty)
Analysis / provenance: https://github.com/haggerty/sphenix-cern-mapping-2022-12-02

These two maps are for the FIELDMAP_TRACKING CDB domain (where the current
measured map lives).  Both are TNtuple "fieldmap" (x:y:z:bx:by:bz:hz, cm/T) on
the production 111^3 / +-110 cm PHField3DCartesian grid — drop-in for the
existing OPERA tracking map.

Both carry the two physical results of the 2022-12-02 mapping:
  - a uniform +0.9 % amplitude scale (Bz(0,0,0) = 1.397 T; OPERA is 1.385 T),
  - a 4.4 mrad horizontal yaw of the field toward -x ("east"); theta_y ~ 0,
    i.e. the magnet is treated as level.

  >>> magfield_rescale = 1.0 with BOTH of these files. <<<
  (The +0.9 % is already baked in.  The 1.0091 factor below is ONLY for the
   bare, uncorrected OPERA map.)


FILES
-----
Which file to register for production is a tracking-group decision.  The two
agree to ~0.84 mT in the tracking volume; they differ only in coverage beyond
r = 90 cm (zero vs. OPERA-filled) and in provenance.  And, pending the survey,
whether the 4.4 mrad yaw should be applied at all is itself open (see CAVEAT).

sphenix_solenoid_measured_smoothed_2022-12-02.root      (8.3 MB)
    The MEASURED field: the phi-averaged, div.B-enforced (r,z) reconstruction
    of the point cloud (real amplitude and z-profile; the ~10 mT per-point
    transverse measurement noise is averaged out) with the measured 4.4 mrad
    yaw reinserted as a rigid rotation.
    Coverage: r <= 90 cm.  Field is ZERO in the cube corners (r > 90 cm), where
    there is no measurement.
    md5 = ee89cad7156467cdc028fa86d84e8a90

sphenix_solenoid_opera_matched_to_mapping_2022-12-02.root   (19.2 MB)
    The OPERA calculation rescaled x1.0091 and yawed 4.4 mrad to match the
    mapping — i.e. OPERA corrected in the spirit of the measurement.
    It is NOT a measured map.  Defined everywhere in the cube (no zeros), so it
    is the option for anything that samples r > 90 cm (field integrals, swimming
    to calorimeter, TPC outer edge).
    Agrees with the measured map to ~0.84 mT in the tracking volume.
    md5 = 574164b77701acb4a635ff58a4086346

CAVEAT (alignment)
------------------
The 4.4 mrad yaw is applied as a rigid rotation.  The field map alone cannot
say whether that rotation is a real magnet yaw or a measurement-frame
(probe/mapper/registration) systematic — that is what the cryostat survey will
decide.  If the survey shows it is a measurement artifact, the yaw should be
dropped (rebuild with THETA_X = 0 in export/makeDeliveredMaps.C).
See the repo's "Systematics" and "Maps delivered to tracking" sections.
