WHY THREE YAW CHOICES (a menu, not one answer)
----------------------------------------------
The 2022-12-02 map shows an apparent horizontal yaw of the field toward -x
("east").  The half-field (half-excitation) cross-check shows this apparent yaw
is NOT a single rigid rotation: a <B_perp> vs Bz fit decomposes it into

   ~2.2 mrad  field-PROPORTIONAL part  (slope; behaves like a real rotation)
 + ~1.4 mT    current-INDEPENDENT part (intercept; does NOT scale with Bz —
              most likely a transverse Hall-probe pedestal, possibly remanence/
              ambient field; indistinguishable from the map alone).

The full-field apparent ~4.4 mrad is the sum of the two at full current.  Since
the field map alone cannot break the magnet-yaw vs measurement-systematic
degeneracy (only the cryostat survey can — it says ~0.17 mrad), we ship the
range and let the tracking group choose:

   yaw = 0.0 mrad  no-tilt / scale-only — consistent with the survey and with
                   the apparent tilt being a measurement systematic.
   yaw = 2.2 mrad  field-proportional yaw, pedestal removed — best single
                   estimate IF a real rotation is assumed (an upper bound; it
                   still includes any probe cross-talk and exceeds the survey).
   yaw = 4.4 mrad  raw apparent yaw — DISFAVOURED: ~1 mrad of it is the additive
                   offset, and the remainder still exceeds the survey.  Provided
                   for continuity with the earlier delivery only.

theta_y ~ 0 in all cases (magnet treated as level).  Rebuild any case with
  root -l -b -q 'export/makeDeliveredMaps.C+(<yawMrad>)'   (see that macro's header).


FILES
-----
For each yaw there are two variants, differing only in coverage beyond r = 90 cm:
  measured_smoothed   the MEASURED field — phi-averaged, div.B-enforced (r,z)
                      reconstruction of the point cloud (real amplitude and
                      z-profile; ~10 mT per-point transverse noise averaged out),
                      yaw reinserted as a rigid rotation.  ZERO in the cube
                      corners (r > 90 cm), where there is no measurement.
  opera_matched       the OPERA calculation rescaled x1.0091 and yawed to match
                      the mapping (NOT a measured map).  Defined everywhere in
                      the cube — use it for anything sampling r > 90 cm (field
                      integrals, swimming to calorimeter, TPC outer edge).
The two agree to ~0.84 mT in the tracking volume at a given yaw.

NOTE on the md5s: ROOT stamps every file with a fresh UUID + timestamp, so the
md5 of a regenerated file differs even when the field VALUES are byte-identical.
These md5s identify THESE delivered files (integrity in transit); they are not a
reproducible-build checksum.  (The yaw=4.4 files have the same field contents,
verified node-by-node, as the 2026-06-09 delivery — only the file md5 differs.)

CAVEAT (alignment) — see also "Half-field analysis" and "Global synthesis" in the repo
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The yaw is applied as a rigid rotation.  The field map alone cannot say whether
the field-proportional part is a real magnet yaw or a measurement-frame
(probe/mapper/registration/cross-talk) systematic, and the half-field cross-check
shows a current-independent ~1.4 mT term on top that is almost certainly
instrumental.  The cryostat survey (which says ~0.17 mrad) is the external check
that would collapse the menu to one map.  Until then the yaw=0.0 (scale-only) and
yaw=2.2 (field-proportional) maps bracket the defensible range; yaw=4.4 is kept
only for continuity and is not recommended.
